MatAnyone2Kit was used as a reference for the staged model decomposition:
https://github.com/flowtyone/MatAnyone2Kit
Reference revision: d85a029870b5149af6aa122cbc13c90db11e5b35

The seven stages are:
encoder, uncertainty estimator, memory matching, readout, decoder,
mask encoder, and object summary.
